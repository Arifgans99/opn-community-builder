# OPN Beginner Guide

## What is OPN?
OPN is a community-driven Web3 ecosystem focused on innovation, accessibility, and decentralized participation.

## Why OPN Matters
- Community reputation system
- Builder-focused ecosystem
- Web3 accessibility
- Decentralized growth

## Ways to Participate
- Join community events
- Support ecosystem discussions
- Create educational content
- Participate in builder activities

## Future Vision
OPN has the potential to become a strong next-generation Web3 ecosystem powered by active community participation.
